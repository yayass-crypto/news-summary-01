President Donald Trump said the war with Iran was "militarily effectively over" after meeting Qatar's prime minister, and Secretary of State Marco Rubio said US economic pressure meant "there's really no need for major combat operations any longer" (AP; NYT). Iran said the Strait of Hormuz, closed for over 250 days, would "never return" to its pre-war status, and designated Argentina's navy and air force as terrorist organisations (Al Jazeera).

Ukraine, Russia and the United States ended two days of talks in Abu Dhabi with no ceasefire but an agreement on a large prisoner-of-war exchange; President Volodymyr Zelenskyy called the first day "constructive and quite substantive" (NPR). NATO scrambled jets after drones strayed into Baltic airspace during Ukrainian strikes on Russia.

Israeli forces killed at least seven people in Gaza, including a father and his 13-year-old son, according to local health officials, as the Gaza and Lebanon truces frayed (Just Security). Israel's new parliament was sworn in with no government formed after a deadlocked election stripped Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu of his majority. The US Justice Department charged three Iran-backed operatives over nearly 20 alleged plots in the US and Europe, including one at RAF Fairford, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent moved to a military base after threats to his life (NYT).

At the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, UN chief Antonio Guterres warned the slow climate response had put the world "on a path to destruction"; 102 heads of state and government were expected, and five countries endorsed doubling homes with rooftop solar by 2030. China's trade surplus passed $1 trillion in the first ten months of 2026, reaching $1.04 trillion, with $105.3 billion in October alone (Customs data). In Sudan, long-range drone strikes hit the power grid as the army pushed into West Kordofan; Delhi's air stayed poor after Diwali, with an air quality index of 299.
