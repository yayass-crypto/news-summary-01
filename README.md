# news-summary-01 — summary of the world's news of 11 November 2026

`main.py` copies `summary.txt` (1,954 characters) to the output file.
The summary was written from the day's reporting (AP, NYT, NPR, Al Jazeera,
Just Security, Customs data), ordered by importance: the Iran war's apparent
end and the Abu Dhabi prisoner exchange; Gaza and Israel's hung parliament;
the US Iran-linked indictments; COP31 in Antalya; China's $1 trillion trade
surplus; Sudan's drone war and Delhi's air. Claims are attributed, not
asserted as fact.

Run: `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py`
